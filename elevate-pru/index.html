Role redesign — task lanes by job family Illustrative

Human–agent teams & autonomy levels
  • L1 Assist — AI suggests; person acts
  • L2 Draft — agent prepares; person approves every item
  • L3 Act within limits — agent completes routine; supervisor samples
  • L4 Multi-step — named business owner; monitoring + stop switch

Move up only on quality evidence; step down after an incident. Builder + AI judge + human review closes the loop.

Technology platform (with Data & Agentic AI teams)
  • People & work data → use-case registry → agentic platform → control layer (identity, checkpoints, judge, audit, stop) → scorecard
  • Standards: NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001; OWASP for LLM/agent security
  • Proven at S&P / DoubleVerify: multi-step engineering under human review; 3 days → same day regulated sign-off
Global footprint

  • India & Ireland — capability centers; Ireland: EU AI Act high-risk employment controls from day one

Intake → tier people impact → joint review (People, CDAIO, Risk, Ethics, Privacy, Compliance, Legal) → high-impact to AI Steering Committee → monitor quality, fairness, people. Represent People on Steering with one shared scorecard.
