Same five steps, every domain

Pick a use case and step through it. The domain owner decides; Data & AI provides the foundation.

    Data platform & enterprise data products

    Centralize the foundation, federate the outcomes

    Centralize (Data & AI owns)Federate (domains own)
    Shared data and ML platform: ingestion, streaming, orchestration, BI, ML and LLM workflowsUse cases, product decisions and the business KPI
    Enterprise metrics and definitions, governed with Finance and approved by the Data Leadership CouncilDomain metrics built on top of the certified definitions
    Listener identity, experimentation and measurement infrastructureExperiments, hypotheses and rollout choices
    Standards, controls and the talent home for data and ML rolesEmbedded analysts and ML engineers working day to day with the domain
    Run shared data as products

    Owner and roadmap

    Each shared data product has a named owner, a roadmap, service expectations and an adoption measure.

    Self-service first

    Curated datasets, a catalog, APIs and certified metrics, so teams do not file tickets for the basics.

    Retire what is redundant

    Every new shared capability names what it replaces. At S&P Global, 8–10 tools were consolidated into shared platforms.

    Data governance, quality & responsible AI

    One operating model for data and AI governance

    Governance is built into the platform, so the governed path is also the fast one. See the controls diagram above ↑.

    AreaWhat it looks like
    Critical data and definitionsNamed owners, quality thresholds, monitoring and escalation for the data that feeds decisions and reporting
    Privacy and permitted useConsent and permitted-use checks run automatically before data reaches a model; sensitive fields scrubbed from logs
    AI standardsApproved tools and models, a risk framework, human oversight sized to listener impact, model monitoring and post-launch measurement
    AuditEvery release carries its evidence: data used, evaluation results, approver, and the KPI it was meant to move

    Lived: I chaired S&P Global's AI Governance Committee (CISO, Risk, Compliance, Legal), reviewed 10+ AI tools, and made privacy, security and regulatory checks required pipeline steps: regulated sign-off went from 3 days to the same day, with 0 autonomous production deploys.

    Applied ML, Agent & LLM platform, AI ways of working

    Make the governed path the easy path

    Agent & LLM platform

    Approved models and tools, reusable patterns (retrieval, agents with tools, evaluation), and support, so teams build on one platform, not beside it.

    Applied ML standards

    One way to develop, evaluate, deploy and monitor models, with communities of practice across product-embedded ML teams.

    AI ways of working

    With People & Culture: skills baselines, paths by role, champions and hackathons, measured by adoption, quality and speed.

    Lived: at S&P Global, 350+ engineers on governed AI in 13 months; engagement 85% → 95% in 3 quarters through a skills assessment and peer pairing; hackathons on automation and customer centricity, the first with 100+ participants. At Prudential, 50+ of 150+ mainframe engineers reskilled into automation.
