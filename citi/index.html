Risk shield: customer test case (illustrative design, public information)

Scenario: A corporate treasury client opening accounts in three markets asks, "Why is our account still not open?" Behind it, an AI extraction agent is drafting the KYC file from submitted documents.

Risk: The draft lists a beneficial owner at 30% who appears in no submitted document, a hallucinated record that could enter the client master and regulatory reporting.

How the system is designed to work: (1) A lineage check requires every extracted field to cite a source page; the unsourced owner is flagged. (2) The field is blocked from the KYC record, and the client receives one precise request for the missing registry extract. (3) A named KYC analyst approves the corrected file before onboarding proceeds. (4) Sources, model version, checks and approver are logged as signed evidence.

Data integrity (design intent): AI quality gates keep ungrounded or hallucinated records out of client-facing records, payments controls and regulatory reports, using data lineage, deterministic checks against the system of record, and approver logs that give non-repudiation for model risk and audit.

Target outcome (illustrative): onboarding files ready in hours rather than days, analysts spending time on judgment instead of re-keying, unsourced facts kept out of the record. Targets to measure in a pilot, not claims.
