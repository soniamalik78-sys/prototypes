The idea in three lines

1 · One register

Every commitment, one owner, one date

Findings, regulatory commitments and remediation milestones live in a single register, each with an accountable owner, success criteria and the evidence needed to close it.

2 · Dependencies first

No surprises on the critical path

Programs are mapped by what they depend on, so a slip in one shows up as a risk to the others weeks before a milestone is missed, not after.

3 · Independent reporting

Same facts, every audience

One evidence-backed data set feeds CIO leadership, the committees and regulator updates, in the format the CAO office sets, so no two reports disagree.

What success looks like in year one: every commitment on track or escalated with a recovery plan, closures accepted by second and third line on first submission, and committee packs produced from one source in days, not weeks.
