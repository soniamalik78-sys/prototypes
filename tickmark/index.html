3

Risk tiers decide how deep each gate goes

Governance that speeds low-risk work up and slows only what needs it.

Tier 1Internal productivity

Staff-facing, no client data leaves the firm, a person uses the answer.

  • Automated evaluation and grounding checks
  • Light UAT with a pilot group
  • Release by AI Solution Delivery
  • Target: idea to production in weeks

Tier 2Client-supporting

Uses client data; a professional reviews every output before it is used.

  • Rubric evaluation on labeled client-like samples
  • Data isolation by client; access reviewed
  • UAT with practitioners on real work
  • Low-confidence items routed to a person

Tier 3Client-facing or standards-bearing

Touches audit evidence, tax positions or client deliverables under professional standards.

  • Full evaluation plus penetration testing
  • Single sign-on and external user access reviewed; sensitive data approved before UAT
  • Professional Practice and Risk review before UAT
  • Audit trail of every recommendation and override
  • Release approved by the Chief AI Officer
