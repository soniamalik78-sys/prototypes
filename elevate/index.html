60-second summary

ELEVATE treats AI adoption as a people transformation first. Each employee gets a role-specific learning path, a nearby champion, clear rules on safe use, and human checkpoints wherever judgment matters. HR, Risk, Compliance and Legal co-own the design so speed and trust grow together.

Why hands-on testing matters

At S&P Global, hands-on testing found code, part legacy and part AI-generated, exposing server directory structure in an error response, and negative DAST scenarios revealed log files exposing details to AI models. Guardrails must be tested, not assumed.

Scenarios ILLUSTRATIVE

A. Anxious claims team illustrative

Pulse survey shows worry about job loss. Leaders share the redesign plan early, champions run clinics, and released hours go to complex cases. Engagement recovers after a planned dip.

B. Shadow AI tool illustrative

Usage of an unapproved chatbot appears. Instead of blocking only, the program offers an approved alternative plus a short safe-use module.

C. Customer letter drafted by AI illustrative

Draft routes to a licensed reviewer; the edit is logged and feeds better templates.
