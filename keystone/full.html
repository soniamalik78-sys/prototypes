The idea in three lines

Guardrails live in the pipeline

Reference architectures, AI provenance, quality gates and human approval run as CI/CD checks, so the governed path is also the fastest one.

One portfolio view of AI

Every AI use case is registered with its objective, owner, risk tier, baseline and KPI, so the steering committee funds by evidence.

Trust is earned with data

People still approve where regulation requires it, but they decide from an objective evidence packet. Decision time and agreement are tracked, so trust in the system is built on evidence.

Forums and escalation

Clear decision rights keep governance at the pace of delivery

Most decisions are made by the pipeline itself. People decide what needs judgment, with a service level, and every exception has an owner and an end date.

Who decidesWhat they decideService levelShare of volume (target)
Pipeline controlsChanges that match a reference architecture and pass every gate: release with standard evidenceMinutes~80%
Design authorityNew patterns, platform deviations and time-boxed exceptions, with a remediation plan5 business days~15%
AI steering committeeFunding, scaling or stopping AI use cases; high-risk AI; new vendors and partnersMonthly, or ad hoc~5%
Technology risk committeeExceptions past their end date, systemic risks, policy changesMonthlyEscalations only

Exceptions expire

Every exception has an owner, a risk rating, a remediation plan and an end date. Overdue exceptions escalate on their own.

Patterns over reviews

When the design authority approves the same deviation twice, it becomes a reference pattern teams can reuse without review.

Value next to risk

The steering committee sees adoption and value beside risk tier, so a use case that adds risk without value is easy to spot.

Human in the loop, by design

People stay accountable. The system earns their trust with evidence.

In a regulated firm, where regulation requires a person to approve, that does not go away. What changes is how the person decides: from reading code line by line to deciding from an objective evidence packet. Every decision is timed and compared with the pipeline's recommendation, so trust grows from data, not from opinion.

The approver can open any line of evidence, and records Approve, Reject or Ask for changes with a reason. Time to decision is logged.

Review depth, by change class
  1. Line-by-line reviewEvery new change class starts here, with full human review.
  2. Evidence-packet reviewThe person decides from the packet and opens detail when something looks off. The floor for regulated and client-facing changes.
  3. Fast-path approvalFor a class with 97%+ agreement over 200+ decisions and no escaped defects: the packet arrives pre-filled, the person still approves, and a sample gets a deep review.

A person approves at every level. Any escaped defect, or agreement dropping below the threshold, moves the class back a level automatically.
