Customer test case (illustrative scenario based on public information)

Scenario: An institutional client asks the AI service assistant, "Has my dividend from last week's corporate action settled?"

Risk: The draft reply says "settled" using a stale record, while the custody ledger shows the payment pending.

Governed path: (1) A grounding check compares the draft against the system of record and flags the mismatch. (2) The reply is blocked and corrected to "pending, expected date shown". (3) Because it concerns client assets, a service specialist approves it before release. (4) Sources, model version and approver are logged in the evidence trail.

Target outcome: ungrounded replies stopped before any client sees them, with response times held near today's levels. These are targets to measure in a pilot, not claims.
