Customer test case (illustrative scenario based on public information)

Scenario: An LP asks the portal assistant, "Why did my capital call amount change?"

Risk: The draft reply cites last quarter's commitment figure, while the current capital call record shows a different amount.

Governed path: (1) An AI quality-control judge compares the draft with the system of record and flags the mismatch. (2) The reply is blocked and rebuilt from the current record. (3) A fund accountant approves it before release. (4) Sources, model version and approver are logged in the evidence trail.

Target outcome: ungrounded answers caught before investors see them, with answer times held near today's levels. These are targets to measure in a pilot, not claims.
