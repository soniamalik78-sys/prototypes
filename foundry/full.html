The idea in three lines

A factory, not a lab

Every use case moves through the same six stages with entry and exit criteria, so a pilot either graduates to production or is retired on purpose.

Build once, reuse everywhere

Agent patterns, prompt templates, evaluation harnesses and guardrails are versioned assets with owners, so each new team starts from a certified pattern.

Ready means measured

Evaluation, reliability, observability and rollback are pipeline gates, with faster, evidence-backed human approval on regulated checks.

Faster, evidence-backed human approval on regulated checks
  1. Full reviewA new class of use case starts with a full human review.
  2. Evidence-packet reviewThe person decides from the packet and opens detail when something looks off. The floor for regulated and client-facing changes.
  3. Fast-path approvalFor a class with a strong track record, the packet arrives pre-filled; the person still approves, and a sample gets a deep review.

A person approves at every level where regulation requires it. Any escaped defect moves the class back a level.

How the team would be organized · proposed

Leaders of leaders, a chapter of architects, and pods of AI engineers

Four groups, each led by a leader of leaders, with architects working across all of them and AI engineering pods aligned to business priorities.

Proposed structure, for illustration
Group 1 · leader of leaders

Agent factory and delivery

  • AI engineering pods aligned to business priorities
  • Own delivery from intake to value realization
  • Report adoption and outcomes on the scorecard
Group 2 · leader of leaders

Reusable assets and patterns

  • Agent patterns, shared components and the prompt-template library
  • Certify, version and retire assets
  • Measure reuse and remove duplication
Group 3 · leader of leaders

Production readiness and reliability

  • Evaluation harness and readiness gates
  • Observability, error budgets and rollback drills
  • Incident reviews that feed new checks
Group 4 · leader of leaders

Responsible AI engineering and enablement

  • Platform controls with Security, Risk, Legal and Compliance
  • Third-party AI reviews, build–buy–partner
  • Training paths for engineers and business teams

Architects as a chapter

Architects sit in the pods but meet as one chapter, so patterns are decided once and reused everywhere.

Pods own outcomes

Each pod owns a KPI, not just a backlog, and reports it against a baseline.

Growing talent

Clear career paths for AI engineers and architects, with pairing and rotation through the readiness group.
