The idea in three lines

Build once, reuse everywhere

Agent patterns, orchestration, connectors and evaluation sets are shared assets. Each new solution starts from them, which cuts duplication and inherits the controls.

Production-ready by design

Reliability, observability, security and Responsible AI are checked at one readiness gate, with evidence, before anything reaches customers or operations.

Measured by value, not launches

Every solution has an owner, a baseline and a business KPI. Adoption and outcomes are tracked after launch, and low-value work is stopped.

Where to go next
Shared assets, not one-off builds

Four agent patterns every new solution starts from

Each pattern is a reusable asset with its components, evaluation set and controls already built in. Teams assemble solutions from them instead of starting from a blank page. "Typical today" describes a typical enterprise, not any specific company.

Human in the loop, by design

People stay accountable. Agents earn trust with evidence.

Where risk or regulation requires a person to approve an agent's action, that does not go away. What changes is how the person decides: from re-checking the agent's work to deciding from an objective evidence packet. Every decision is timed and compared with the agent's recommendation, so trust grows from data, not from opinion.

The analyst can open any line of evidence and records Approve, Change or Reject with a reason. Time to decision is logged.

Review depth, by action type
  1. Full reviewEvery new agent and action type starts here: a person checks the agent's work in full before anything is sent or changed.
  2. Evidence-packet reviewThe person decides from the packet and opens detail when something looks off. The floor for actions that affect customers or money.
  3. Fast-path approvalFor an action type with 97%+ agreement over 200+ decisions and no incidents: the packet arrives pre-filled, a person still approves, and a sample gets a deep review.

Where regulation requires a person, a person decides at every level. Any incident, or agreement dropping below the threshold, moves the action type back a level automatically.
