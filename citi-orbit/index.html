Architecture & developer experience

Experience: KYC extraction assistant and analyst approval console.
Builder agents: requirements drafting, AI-assisted code, context-aware tests, release notes.
Judge agents: score each change against a custom rubric (accuracy, security, grounding); reviewers decide from that evidence.
Evaluation: golden datasets, negative DAST scenarios, regression suites run on every merge.
Governance: risk tiers, approved-tool inventory, named human approvers for regulated paths, evidence trail kept for audit.
Ledger and data safety (design intent): AI quality gates block ungrounded or hallucinated records before they reach the KYC client record, payments controls and regulatory reporting; data lineage, signed evidence and approver logs for non-repudiation; deterministic checks against the system of record.
Platform: shared COO AI platform: approved models, reusable RAG and evaluation, KYC system of record, model risk inventory.
