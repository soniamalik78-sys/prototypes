The idea in three lines

Agents do the routine checks

Security, risk, compliance, legal, and audit agents review every use case and vendor tool, from planning through production.

Experts find a path forward

When agents escalate, a senior team proposes a way to proceed, within a service level, instead of a plain yes or no.

Evidence is continuous

Every check leaves an auditable record, so exams and audits draw on live evidence, not a point-in-time review.

Where to go next
Enterprise-wide coverage

Three tiers, three different risk profiles

The same agents cover all three tiers, but what they look for changes. Vendor AI needs the most attention, because the AI runs outside the company's own environment. "Before" describes a typical enterprise, not any specific company.

Try it

The agent desk

Pick a sample submission, one from each tier. Five governance agents review it, an expert proposes paths forward, and the decision lands in the evidence ledger.

Coach, not judge

Clear decision rights keep governance at the pace of delivery

Agents handle the routine. People handle judgment. Every escalation ends with a way to proceed, or a clear explanation of what would make one possible.

"No" needs a path

An escalation closes only with a path forward, or with the specific change that would unlock one. Tracked as a metric.

Patterns over reviews

When the experts solve a case twice, it becomes a reusable control or pattern that agents can apply next time.

Literacy, not gatekeeping

Guidance, patterns, and office hours build governance literacy, so teams arrive with designs that already pass.

Under the hood

Agents wired into the systems that already hold the evidence

The agents connect to the delivery pipeline, the AI gateway, procurement, and existing risk, compliance, security, and audit platforms, so oversight is continuous rather than a meeting.

Governing the governance agents

  • Each agent is evaluated against expert decisions before it acts on its own
  • A weekly human sample of agent decisions, with agreement tracked
  • Every agent finding explains its reasoning and cites its evidence
  • Agents recommend; only people reject a submission

Continuous, not point-in-time

  • Re-scan when a model, prompt, vendor release, or contract changes
  • Drift and misuse monitoring after deployment
  • Vendor release notes and terms watched for new AI features
  • Evidence ready for regulatory exams and audits at any time

Security and supply chain

  • Model artifacts verified by hash and scanned for backdoors
  • An AI bill of materials for models, datasets, and libraries
  • Prompt-injection and data-leakage test suites
  • Sub-processor changes flagged for vendor AI

Regulatory map

  • NIST AI RMF functions tagged on every control
  • NAIC model bulletin program and third-party expectations
  • NYDFS guidance on AI in underwriting and pricing
  • SR 11-7-style validation for models that inform decisions

Design targets: routine Tier 2 decisions the same day; Tier 3 vendor decisions within 5 business days; agent findings agree with expert reviewers at least 90% of the time before agents act alone.
