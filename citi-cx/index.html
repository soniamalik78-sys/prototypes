Customer test case: a disputed charge (illustrative design, public information)

Scenario: A cardholder opens the app and types, "I don't recognize a $212 charge from last night, and I need that money for rent."

How the assistant is designed to work:

  1. Intent detection: classified as "unrecognized charge" with urgency; the merchant, date and amount are pulled from the account, not guessed.
  2. Grounded answer with citations: the reply shows the transaction record and the dispute policy it relies on; if no source supports a statement, it is not said.
  3. Act or hand off: the agent may lock the card and open a dispute only within approved limits and with the customer's confirmation. Fraud signals, high amounts, vulnerability cues or low model confidence route to a specialist with full context (transcript, steps taken, evidence), so the customer never repeats the story or waits in a hold loop.
  4. Evals before and after launch: golden conversations scored for grounding, policy accuracy, tone and correct hand-off; latency and cost per conversation tracked; every action logged for Risk, Fraud and audit.

Data integrity (design intent): AI quality gates keep hallucinated amounts, dates or policy claims out of customer replies and account records, using lineage to the system of record and approver logs.

Target outcomes (illustrative, to be baselined): +5–10 pts CSAT on disputes, higher first-contact resolution, 20–30% containment on low-risk intents, 15–25% lower cost per contact. Targets to measure in a pilot, not claims.
