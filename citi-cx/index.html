A disputed charge, resolved before you call

Cardholder sees an unfamiliar $212 charge the night before rent is due.

BEFORE
Finds a phone number, waits on hold, repeats the story to two people, then waits days for a letter.
AFTER (DESIGNED EXPERIENCE)
Citi notices the unusual charge, sends an in-app alert (“Did you make this purchase?”), and with one tap locks the card and opens a dispute. The answer cites the transaction and the dispute policy; a decision date is shown.

When AI acts: The assistant may lock the card and open a dispute within approved limits and only with the customer's confirmation.
When a person takes over: Fraud signals, high amounts, vulnerability cues or low confidence go to a specialist with the full transcript and evidence, so the customer never repeats the story or sits in a hold loop.

RESPONSIBLE AI BUILT INTO THE DESIGN
  • GuardrailsAnswers grounded in the account and policy with citations; unsupported amounts or promises are blocked before the customer sees them.
  • Fraud signalsFraud models decide whether the case is eligible for self-service or goes straight to a specialist.
  • Model governanceEvery action logged with model version, sources and hand-off reason for Risk, Fraud and audit.

Measured by: Customer effort score, first-contact resolution, cost per contact (targets illustrative).

Rewards and benefits you already have, at the right moment

Customer books flights with a Citi card but never uses the card's travel protections or bonus categories.

BEFORE
Benefits sit in a PDF; points expire or go unused; the customer feels the card is not worth the fee.
AFTER (DESIGNED EXPERIENCE)
At booking, Citi reminds the customer which card earns the most and what protection already applies. Before points lapse, a relevant way to use them is offered. One relationship view across card, checking and rewards.

When AI acts: Reminders and suggestions run automatically within agreed frequency caps and the customer's consent and channel preferences.
When a person takes over: Anything that changes the account or fees, or a complaint, goes to a person with context.

RESPONSIBLE AI BUILT INTO THE DESIGN
  • GuardrailsConsent, frequency caps and suppression for customers in hardship; claims about benefits checked against the actual terms.
  • ComplianceMarketing and UDAAP review built into templates, not added at the end.
  • Model governanceRecommendation models monitored for drift and relevance; customers can see why they got a suggestion.

Measured by: Benefit utilization, NPS, products per relationship (targets illustrative).

Lending offers based on your relationship, not a cold pitch

A long-time checking and card customer is planning a home renovation.

BEFORE
Generic mailers with offers that do not match; the customer applies elsewhere without knowing Citi would lend.
AFTER (DESIGNED EXPERIENCE)
With the customer's permission, Citi uses the relationship it already has to show a relevant, pre-qualified option in-app, with a plain-language explanation of the terms and why it fits.

When AI acts: AI personalizes timing and presentation; credit decisions follow Citi's approved credit models and policies.
When a person takes over: Declines and borderline cases come with clear reasons and a route to a lending specialist.

RESPONSIBLE AI BUILT INTO THE DESIGN
  • Credit explainabilityEach decision carries reason codes that can be shown to the customer as adverse-action notices.
  • Fair lendingFair-lending testing of models and offer targeting before launch and on an ongoing basis, with Compliance and Legal.
  • Model governanceCredit and marketing models under model risk management, with validation and monitoring.

Measured by: Products per relationship, conversion, NPS (targets illustrative).
