What I believe

  • No AI chatbot stacked in front of a phone tree. AI resolves the issue or hands off to a person with full context.
  • AI never makes the credit decision. Approved credit models do; AI explains and personalizes.
  • Every proactive message must save the customer time or money, or it doesn't go out.

Three flagship journeys

A disputed charge, resolved before you call

BEFORE
Hold queue, the story repeated twice, then days of waiting for a letter.
AFTER (DESIGNED EXPERIENCE)
Citi flags the charge in-app; one tap locks the card and opens a dispute, with policy cited and a decision date shown.

AI acts: Lock the card and open a dispute, within approved limits and with the customer's confirmation. A person takes over: Fraud signals, high amounts, vulnerability cues or low confidence go to a specialist with the full transcript, no hold loop.

Rewards and benefits you already have, at the right moment

BEFORE
Benefits buried in a PDF; points expire; the card feels not worth the fee.
AFTER (DESIGNED EXPERIENCE)
At booking, Citi shows which card earns most and what protection already applies; before points lapse, it offers a relevant way to use them.

AI acts: Reminders within consent, channel preferences and frequency caps. A person takes over: Fee changes or complaints.

Lending offers based on your relationship, not a cold pitch

BEFORE
Generic mailers; the customer borrows elsewhere, unaware Citi would lend.
AFTER (DESIGNED EXPERIENCE)
With permission, Citi uses the existing relationship to show a relevant pre-qualified option in-app, with terms and fit explained plainly.

AI acts: Personalizes timing and explanation; approved credit models decide. A person takes over: Declines and borderline cases, with clear reasons.
