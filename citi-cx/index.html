    What makes this hard at Citi

    The demo is the easy part

    • Fraud-loss exposure: holding back a disputed amount before the investigation ends needs limits Fraud and Credit Risk agree to, by segment and amount.
    • Two businesses, two systems: the card autopay and the checking account sit in different businesses and platforms; adjusting one from the other needs consent and a shared customer view.
    • Merchant updates aren't universal: network account-updater services cover many recurring merchants, not all, so the message has to say which bills moved and which didn't.
    • Model risk: the fraud and intent models need model-risk validation and ongoing monitoring before an agent may act on them.
    • Explanations are a requirement: every decision needs a reason the customer, a complaint team and an examiner can all read.
